Privacy & Data Protection

Local Data Residency: All imported contact CSVs, research evidence, and draft emails remain strictly on your local disk in output/ and data/. No prospect contact details are transmitted to external marketing services or third-party servers.

Credential Protection: Google OAuth client secrets and user tokens are stored solely in filesystem paths specified by local environment variables (GMAIL_CREDENTIALS_PATH, GMAIL_TOKEN_PATH). They are never accepted, echoed, or modified through the web interface.

Path to Public Deployment

Before this testing application can be deployed to a public URL or used beyond local loopback:

  • Multi-user authentication (e.g. session auth, OAuth2) must be implemented.
  • A secure reverse-proxy (e.g. Nginx, Cloudflare) with HTTPS/TLS must terminate traffic.
  • Legal Privacy Policy and Terms of Service documents must be formally reviewed and published.
  • Google OAuth app verification must be approved for restricted Gmail API scopes.
